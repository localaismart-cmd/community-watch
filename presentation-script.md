# 🛡️ Community Watch — Presentation Script
## Senior Citizens Club · Lakes Entrance

**Total time:** 5-7 minutes

---

### 👋 Introduction (30 sec)

*"Good morning everyone! My name is [Name] and I'm a local from Lakes Entrance. I built something for our community that helps keep us all safe and connected."*

### 🛡️ What is Community Watch? (1 min)

*"Community Watch is a free app for your phone that lets neighbours share safety information — but the beauty is, it's designed to be really simple. You don't need an account, you don't need a password. You just enter our postcode 3909 and you're in."*

### 📱 Show Them (2 min)

*"Let me show you how it works. If anyone has their phone, you can follow along:"*

1. Take out your phone
2. Type: **communitywatchlakesentrance.com.au**
3. Enter postcode **3909**
4. Tap **Verify & Join**
5. Show the **About page** (where the app opens)
6. Tap the **Report tab**
7. Show the **000 button**

*"See? No accounts, no passwords. Just enter 3909 and you're connected to your neighbourhood."*

### 👴 Why It's Perfect for Seniors (1 min)

*"This app was designed with seniors in mind:*

- *One tap to call 000 in an emergency*
- *No typing — you can use the 🎤 microphone button to speak your report*
- *It sits on your phone like any other app*
- *You get notified if something's happening in your street*
- *Works in 25 languages including Greek, Italian, and Vietnamese"*

### ❓ Answer Questions (1 min)

*"I know some of you might be thinking: 'Is it safe? Will it use my data?'*

- *It's completely FREE. No charges ever.*
- *Your location is NOT tracked. No background tracking.*
- *All the rules are in the Terms page — we follow Australian privacy laws.*
- *Every alert expires after 24 hours — no clutter."*

### 🙏 Close (30 sec)

*"I'd love for you all to give it a try. Just go to **communitywatchlakesentrance.com.au** on your phone, enter postcode 3909, and you're connected to your community.*

*And please, share it with your neighbours too. The more of us on it, the safer we all are."*

> **Hand out the flyers here!**
