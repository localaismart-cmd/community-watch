❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🚑 What to do in an emergency?+
Call Triple Zero (000) immediately. Do NOT wait. Tell the operator your location and what's happening. After calling 000, you can use the Report tab to alert your neighbours. This app is NOT a replacement for emergency services.
📋 How do I report an alert?+
Tap the Report tab, choose the alert type (Emergency/Be Alert/Community), write a title and description, add the location, then tap Submit Alert. Your neighbours will be notified!
🛡️ Is this a replacement for police?+
No. Community Watch is a neighbourhood communication tool. It is NOT a replacement for Triple Zero (000), police, fire, or ambulance services. Always call 000 in an emergency.
🔒 Is my location tracked?+
No. Your location is only used when you submit an alert or join the community. There is NO background tracking. All data stays on your device.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Can kids use this app?+
Young people can use the app under adult supervision. If you feel unsafe, tell a trusted adult immediately or call 000. Kids Helpline: 1800 55 1800.
🛡️ How does moderation work?+
Local moderators review content and can remove inappropriate alerts or users. Moderators review community content but does NOT make moderation decisions. If you see something inappropriate, submit a report.
