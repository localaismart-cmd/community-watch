# ❓ Community Watch — Q&A Cheat Sheet
## Senior Citizens Club Presentation

---

### Q: Is it really free?
**A:** Yes! Completely free. No charges, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. Ever.

### Q: Do I need an email or account?
**A:** No! Just enter postcode **3909** and you're in. No sign-ups, no passwords.

### Q: Will it track my location?
**A:** No. Your location is NEVER tracked. The app only asks for your location when you submit an alert — and even then, it's optional.

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** Yes. Your data stays on YOUR phone. We don't collect or share anything.

### Q: I'm not good with technology. Is it hard to use?
**A:** It's very simple. Open the link, enter 3909, and you're connected. If you get stuck, any young family member or neighbour can help you set it up in 30 seconds.

### Q: What if I don't have a smartphone?
**A:** Unfortunately you need a smartphone. But most people in our community have one. If you need help getting set up, just ask me after the presentation.

### Q: Will this replace calling 000?
**A:** NEVER. This app is NOT a replacement for emergency services. Always call 000 in an emergency. This app simply helps you share information with your neighbours.

### Q: Can I use this to check on my neighbours?
**A:** Yes! If you haven't seen a neighbour in a day or two, you can post a Community alert asking if anyone has seen them. It's a great way to keep an eye on each other.

### Q: What about during fire season?
**A:** This is especially useful during fire season. If you see smoke, call 000 first, then use the app to warn your street. Neighbours on the ground see fires before official warnings come out.

### Q: I only need it for emergencies. Can I keep it on my phone and not use it daily?
**A:** Absolutely! Install it, set it up, and leave it on your phone. You'll only get notifications when someone in the neighbourhood posts an alert.

### Q: Can I get the flyer for a friend who isn't here?
**A:** Yes! I have flyers with me. Take one for your neighbour, your family member, anyone who'd benefit.
