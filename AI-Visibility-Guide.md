# AI Engine Visibility — Multi-Platform Submission Guide
## For All Local AI Smart Projects

### Why This Matters
Your site appears in Google Search but NOT in AI engines (Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot) because they use older training data. You need to manually submit your site to each AI engine to speed up discovery.

### Submission Checklist

| AI Engine | Submit Here | Time to Appear | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔍 **Google Search** | ✅ Already done via Search Console | ✅ Already found | ✅ **Done** |
| 🧠 **Gemini** | Not directly submittable — appears via Google Search over time | 1-3 months | ⏳ Auto |
| 🤖 **ChatGPT** | https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-search-opt-out/ | Must be indexed by Bing first | ⏳ Auto |
| 🔎 **Perplexity** | https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/submit-your-site | 1-2 weeks | 🟡 **To Do** |
| 🌐 **Bing** | https://www.bing.com/webmasters | 1-2 weeks | 🟡 **To Do** |
| ⚡ **Yandex** | https://webmaster.yandex.com | 1 month | 🟢 Optional |

### Step-by-Step Submission

#### 1. Submit to Bing (Fastest Win)
1. Go to **https://www.bing.com/webmasters**
2. Sign in with Microsoft account
3. Add site URL → Verify via meta tag (like Google)
4. Submit sitemap: https://yoursite.com/sitemap.xml
5. **Effect:** Helps ChatGPT + Copilot find you faster

#### 2. Submit to Perplexity
1. Go to **https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/submit-your-site**
2. Enter your URL
3. Get indexed within 1-2 weeks
